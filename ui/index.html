Our platform never labels a claim as simply True or False. Instead, it combines evidence from four independent verification engines to generate an explainable Trust Report, empowering users to make informed decisions.

analytics Scoring Methodology

Trust Score Interpretation

Evaluation framework based on cross-engine evidence convergence.

Low Confidence (0 – 39) Moderate Confidence (40 – 79) High Confidence (80 – 100)
Low Confidence 0 – 39

Evidence collected is limited, inconsistent, or insufficient to strongly support the claim.

Moderate Confidence 40 – 79

Some evidence supports the claim, but additional verification or corroboration is recommended.

High Confidence 80 – 100

Multiple independent verification engines consistently support the submitted claim, resulting in a strong level of confidence.

The Trust Score represents the strength and consistency of evidence collected from four independent verification engines. It is designed to help users make informed decisions and should not be interpreted as an absolute True or False verdict.
